1. THE SHORT VERSION

- Incus Multicodec is free. Its own code is open source under the MIT licence (section 6), at
  https://github.com/incustechsupport/incus-multicodec
- It is provided "as is", without warranty of any kind (section 3).
- It switches the Bluetooth adapter you choose over to its own driver (WinUSB). While it has the
  adapter, Windows can't use it: if that is your PC's built-in Bluetooth, Windows Bluetooth is off
  until you give it back from the tray menu (Bluetooth dongle > Give back to Windows), or uninstall
  the app, which gives it back.
- Bluetooth mice and keyboards keep only their newest pairing. Pairing one with Incus Multicodec
  replaces its pairing with Windows, so after giving the adapter back you need to pair it with
  Windows again.
- It sends nothing about you anywhere, except: an update check (twice a day, can be turned off), and
  bug reports, only when you send one. See section 4.


2. WHAT THE APP CHANGES ON YOUR PC

- The Bluetooth adapter you pick is switched to Microsoft's WinUSB driver (Windows asks for
  administrator permission). Uninstalling, or "Give back to Windows", switches it back.
- Optionally: an entry that starts the app when you sign in, and a scheduled task that runs it as
  administrator (only if you turn on "Mice and keyboards work in admin windows").
- Settings, pairing keys and a log file are kept in %LOCALAPPDATA%\windows-bluetooth-multicodec.
  Uninstalling leaves this folder; delete it to remove your pairings.

4. PRIVACY

The full privacy notice is at https://software.incustech.app/multicodec/privacy/. In short:

- Update checks fetch https://software.incustech.app/multicodec/latest.json; updates are
  downloaded from GitHub. Like any web request, these reveal your IP address to the server they go
  to. Turn checks off under Updates > Check automatically.
- Bug reports are sent only when you press "Send report". They contain what you type, your email
  if you give it, a summary of your PC (app and Windows version, Bluetooth adapters, codec and
  settings) and, if you leave the box ticked, the end of the app's log (device names, with
  Bluetooth addresses masked). "See what's sent" shows the exact contents first.
- Everything else (settings, pairing keys, the log) stays on your PC.
